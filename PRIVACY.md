# Rosewash Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Rosewash customizes the appearance of web pages using color palettes, optional local fonts, and optional reading layouts.

## Page access

Rosewash processes page elements and their styles on your device to change colors, typography, and layout. It reads the current page address to apply site-specific settings and display the current site in its popup. It does not record a browsing history, store page contents, or send page contents or browsing activity to the developer. Page styling state is temporary and lasts only while the page is open.

## Saved preferences and browser sync

Rosewash saves your enabled state, appearance mode, palettes, selected font names, layout preferences, and excluded website hostnames using `chrome.storage.sync`. These preferences remain until you change or reset them. If your browser enables extension settings synchronization, its sync provider may receive and synchronize these preferences across your devices. In Google Chrome, this service is provided by Google and is subject to your browser settings and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Rosewash does not operate a server that receives these preferences. The developer cannot access your browser sync storage.

## Local fonts

The optional font picker requests browser permission to list fonts installed on your device. Rosewash uses the list locally to let you choose fonts and saves only your selected font names in extension settings. It does not upload font files or the complete list of installed fonts. Custom fonts are disabled by default.

## Data use and sharing

Rosewash contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or remote executable code. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to customizing page appearance. The extension does not send user data to the developer or other services; the browser-managed settings synchronization described above is the only synchronization used by Rosewash.

## Your controls

You can disable Rosewash globally, exclude individual websites, turn off custom fonts, and edit or reset saved preferences from its settings. Reset restores the default settings and clears the excluded-site list and selected font names. You can manage synchronization and local font permissions in your browser. Uninstalling Rosewash removes the extension from that browser; management of any synchronized copy is controlled by the browser's sync service.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use https://github.com/Yalyenea/rosewash/issues. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private information. Information you choose to submit to GitHub is handled under GitHub's privacy policy.
